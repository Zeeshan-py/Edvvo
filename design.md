# Edvvo Design

Audience: Pakistani secondary and higher-secondary students.
Purpose: find relevant material, then move into a focused study session.
Direction: a tactile student desk with short functional copy.

## Shared System
Use the named tokens in src/styles/studio.css.
Warm ivory canvas, ink typography, cobalt actions, muted subject-card colors.
Manrope display and body; upright headings, strong weight, tight display tracking.
Layout: compact navigation, split illustrated introduction, search and class shortcuts,
four illustrated resource cards, study tools, expandable learning notes, compact footer.
Library pages use controls above real resources. Content pages use a quiet reading layout.

## Motion
One-time staggered entrances, limited pointer tilt, card artwork movement on hover.
No autoplay carousels, endless bouncing, flashing, or fake progress.
Respect reduced motion, keyboard focus, touch input and small screens.
CSS illustrations represent study materials; they are not simulated user activity.

## Content
Use direct action labels. No fabricated testimonials, statistics, institutional
endorsements or claims of AdSense approval. Keep detailed learning material on
resource pages and expandable tips. External source links must be identifiable.
Unavailable material is labeled honestly. No ad-like buttons or deceptive downloads.

## Page Map
Home: discover resources and study tools.
Explore: board, class and material controls with the existing resources.
Workspace: planner, timer, roadmaps, guides and calculator.
About and contact: purpose, support routes and resource feedback.
Policy pages: retain policy details and keep links visible in the footer.
Academic fields, books and notes: preserve routes and existing content.
